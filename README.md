# OpenRCT2 Ride & Stall Throughput / Price Notifier

A plugin for **OpenRCT2** that automatically notifies you whenever guests think a ride or stall is too expensive or too cheap, or when a ride's guest throughput falls below expectations.

The goal is to help you focus on building and managing your park without constantly checking individual rides and stalls to make sure they're performing well and making money.

It provides useful management feedback without feeling like a cheat, keeping the gameplay experience balanced and enjoyable.

Highly configurable though in-game settings.
